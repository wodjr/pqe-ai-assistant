PQE Assistant v0.5.0

Procurement Quality Engineering AI Assistant

Official User Operation Manual & Field Guide

1. Overview & Lifecycle

The PQE AI Assistant supports Quality Engineers throughout supplier qualification, manufacturing process evaluations, and shop-floor line walkthroughs.

End-to-End Workflow:
Import Checklist ➔ Create Audit ➔ AI Pre-Audit Prep ➔ Live Smart Audit Studio ➔ Onsite Findings ➔ 8D CARs ➔ Qualification Sign-off

2. Pre-Audit Preparation

2.1 Importing Excel Checklists

  1. Go to Checklists in the top navigation.
  2. Upload your company's standard audit Excel workbook (.xlsx / .xls).
  3. The system parses sections, questions, references, and guidance while preserving the original file.

2.2 Creating an Audit & Generating Agenda

  1. Click Audits ➔ + New Audit and fill in supplier details.
  2. On the Audit Hub, use the AI Audit Agenda card to generate a day-by-day 08:00–17:00 time-slotted schedule, opening notes, and pre-arrival document requests.
  3. Upload the supplier's completed pre-audit self-assessment Excel to receive an automatic AI Pre-Audit Briefing flagging all risk areas.

3. Onsite Smart Audit Note Taker (Live Studio)

Plaud-Style Walkthrough Studio: Designed for mobile/desktop one-handed use during live line walks and interviews.

3.1 Live Recording & Voice Markers

  • Tap Start Smart Audit and confirm the recording consent notice.
  • Speech-to-Text: Real-time streaming transcription with continuous auto-save to browser storage.
  • Voice Markers: Speak phrases like "PQE note", "Potential finding", "Take action", "Good practice", or tap the colored quick-chips to mark timestamps without typing.

3.2 Non-Interruptive Photo Capture

  • Tap 📷 Add Photo on the sticky recorder bar.
  • Take or attach a picture of shop floor evidence, parts, or gauges.
  • AI suggests potential manufacturing risks (poka-yoke absence, orientation error, calibration expiry) without pausing the audio recording.

3.3 Smart AI Notes & Prioritized Prompts

  • Smart Notes Tab: Summarizes the discussion into Statements, Supplier Commitments, Process Controls, Documents Mentioned, and Risks.
  • AI Question Assistant: Quietly recommends the top 1–3 probing questions classified as CRITICAL, IMPORTANT, or FOLLOW_UP.
  • Checklist Matrix: Live comparison categorizing items into COVERED, PARTIALLY_COVERED, NOT_COVERED, or OBJECTIVE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED.
  • End-of-Session 13-Point Summary: Produces a full closing meeting draft.

5. Findings, 8D CARs & Reports

5.1 Findings Log & 8D CARs

  • Classify findings as MAJOR, MINOR, OBSERVATION, or OFI.
  • Use 8D workflow for corrective actions: Containment ➔ Root Cause ➔ Corrective Action ➔ Effectiveness Verification ➔ Auditor Closure.

5.2 Qualification & Print-Ready Report

  • Lead auditor issues formal qualification: APPROVE, CONDITIONAL, or REJECT.
  • Click Generate Report ➔ Print / Save PDF for client-ready executive PDF reports.

6. Phone-to-Mac Transfer (ZIP Backup)

Backup Rule: In the prototype, all data is in browser IndexedDB. Go to Settings ➔ Export with Photos (.zip) to bundle all audit data + photo blobs into a single file for AirDrop/iCloud transfer.

7. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Rules

  • AI suggestions are strictly advisory and never auto-approve findings or checklist items.
  • Only authorized human auditors can approve findings, close CARs, and sign qualification decisions.
